Mode: Operate. Existing-world extension, following DESIGN.md; no new brand direction or comp round.

FORM: Central settings library beside the workbench on desktop; stacked on narrow screens. Saved identity is collapsible, preview and results are explicit. Upload and style variants are managed only in settings.

Truth constraints: Connection test is read-only. Video and generated style images require explicit cost/upload confirmation. Uploaded styles are categories, not automatic transformations. Preserve original avatars, project notes, images, narration, history and browser drafts. Use durable generation IDs and safe reuse.
